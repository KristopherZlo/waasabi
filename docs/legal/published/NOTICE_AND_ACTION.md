# Notice & Action (Illegal content reports)

**Website:** The Hub (deployment URL configured by the operator)  
**Effective date:** January 28, 2026  
**Last updated:** January 28, 2026  
**Version:** 1.0

This page explains how to report potentially illegal content on The Hub and how we respond.

## 1. Where to send a notice

You can report content:

- via the in-app **Report** action on the relevant content page (where available); or
- by email: `zloydeveloper.info@gmail.com`.

## 2. What to include

To help us review quickly, include:

- a link (URL) to the content and/or its identifier;
- why you believe the content is illegal (and the legal basis, if known);
- your role (affected person, rights holder, representative, etc.);
- your contact details for follow-up; and
- supporting evidence, if any.

Please include a good-faith statement such as:

> I submit this notice in good faith and believe the information in it is accurate and complete.

If you act on behalf of someone else, include the basis for your authority.

## 3. How we handle notices

We aim to:

- perform an initial review within a reasonable timeframe (target: **72 hours**);
- review notices as soon as reasonably possible;
- request clarification if needed;
- take appropriate action where required (for example, removal or restricting access); and
- preserve evidence where necessary for safety or legal compliance.

We may take action based on our Community Guidelines and Terms of Service in addition to legal requirements.

## 4. Notifications and reasons

Where required by law and where feasible, we may provide affected users with information about content restriction decisions and the main reasons for those decisions.

## 5. Appeals

If you believe we made an incorrect decision, you can appeal by emailing `zloydeveloper.info@gmail.com` with:

- a link to the affected content;
- your account information (if applicable); and
- why you believe the decision was wrong.

## 6. Abuse of the reporting channel

Submitting intentionally false or abusive reports may result in enforcement action (for example, limiting reporting features or account suspension).

## 7. Recordkeeping and disclosures

We may keep records of notices and actions for security, accountability, and legal compliance. We may share information with authorities if required by law.
