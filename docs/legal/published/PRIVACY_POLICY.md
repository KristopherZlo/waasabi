## 3. Personal data we process

We process the following categories of personal data (depending on how you use the Service):

| Category | Examples | Required | Typical visibility |
|---|---|---:|---|
| Account data | email, display name, password hash, email verification timestamp | Yes | Not public (except display name and profile link) |

We process personal data for the following purposes under the GDPR:

| Purpose | Examples | Legal basis |
|---|---|---|
| Provide the Service | create accounts, authenticate users, display content | Performance of a contract (Terms) |
| Safety and abuse prevention | rate limits, moderation, spam detection, reporting systems | Legitimate interests (keeping the Service safe) and/or legal obligation |

## 5. Automated moderation and decisions

The Service may use automated systems to assist with moderation and abuse prevention, for example:

- text heuristics to flag spam-like content;
- weighted user reports that can temporarily restrict visibility of content; and
- (if enabled) scanning uploaded images for sexual content using AWS Rekognition in the configured region.

These tools may result in temporary restrictions (for example, content being marked "pending" or "hidden"). You can request human review and appeal moderation actions by contacting `zloydeveloper.info@gmail.com`.

We do not make decisions that produce legal or similarly significant effects solely by automated means without the possibility of review.

## 8. Retention

We keep personal data only as long as necessary for the purposes described above. In practice:

- Account data is kept while your account is active and until you request deletion.
- Public User Content may remain available to preserve discussion threads; where feasible, we can delete or anonymize content upon request, unless retention is required by law or for legitimate safety reasons.
- Session data expires after a period of inactivity (configured as 120 minutes), but technical records may remain until routine cleanup.
- Reports, moderation logs, and audit logs are retained as needed for safety, enforcement, and legal compliance, and may be kept longer if required for investigations or legal obligations.
