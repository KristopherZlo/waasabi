This Cookie Policy explains what cookies are and which cookies The Hub uses.

## 1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files that a website stores on your device via your browser. Similar technologies (like local storage) can serve similar purposes.

## 2. Cookies we use

We currently use only **essential** cookies required to operate the Service (for example, to keep you signed in and protect against abuse).

We do **not** use analytics or marketing cookies at this time.

| Name | Provider | Purpose | Expiry | Essential |
|---|---|---|---|---:|
| Session cookie (name varies by configuration) | The Hub | Authentication and session management | ~120 minutes of inactivity | Yes |
| `XSRF-TOKEN` (if set) | The Hub | CSRF protection | Session / short-lived | Yes |

Note: we store your cookie banner choice in your browser's local storage under `the-hub.cookie.consent.v1`. This is not a cookie.

## 3. Managing cookies

You can control cookies through your browser settings. If you block essential cookies, the Service may not work correctly (for example, you may not be able to sign in).

## 4. Changes to this policy

If we start using additional cookies (for example, analytics), we will update this policy and, where required, request your consent.
