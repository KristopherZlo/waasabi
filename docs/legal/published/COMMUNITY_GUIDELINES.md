# Community Guidelines

**Website:** The Hub (deployment URL configured by the operator)  
**Effective date:** January 28, 2026  
**Last updated:** January 28, 2026  
**Version:** 1.0

The Hub is a forum for original creative projects and collaboration. Please help keep it safe, useful, and respectful.

These Guidelines apply to all content and activity on the Service.

## 1. Respect and safety

- No harassment, bullying, or targeted abuse.
- No threats or incitement to violence.
- No hate speech or discrimination.

## 2. Privacy

- Do not post other people's personal data without a lawful basis or consent (no doxxing).
- Assume posts may be public and indexed by search engines.
- Do not post sensitive personal data (passport, home address, bank details, etc.).

## 3. Illegal and harmful content

- Do not post illegal content or instructions that facilitate illegal activity.
- Defamation and knowingly false accusations are not allowed.
- CSAM is strictly prohibited. If we become aware of such content, we will remove it and may report it to competent authorities.

## 4. Academic integrity

- No buying or selling assignments, exam cheating, plagiarism, or sharing confidential exam materials.
- Learning help and discussion are allowed; do not request or provide cheating services.

## 5. Spam, scams, and security

- No spam, scams, phishing, or misleading promotions.
- No malware or harmful code.
- Do not attempt to bypass security controls or rate limits.

## 6. Copyright and IP

- Do not post content you do not have rights to use.
- If you are a rights holder, you can report infringement: `/legal/notice-and-action`.

## 7. Reporting and moderation

If you see something that may violate these rules or the law, report it using the in-app reporting tools (where available) or by email: `zloydeveloper.info@gmail.com`.

We may remove content and take enforcement action (warnings, suspensions, bans) to keep the Service safe and lawful.
