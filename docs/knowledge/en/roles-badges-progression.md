# Roles, badges, and account progression

## Roles
- User: can view content, publish, comment, follow, report, save, and upvote.
- Maker: all user abilities plus the ability to leave structured reviews.
- Support: handles support tickets and replies.
- Moderator: moderates content and can ban or unban users.
- Admin: full access, including role changes and badge management.

Roles are hierarchical: higher roles inherit the abilities of lower roles.

## Account progression (Maker)
- Maker promotion is automatic when your posts consistently earn strong upvote scores.
- The system uses a dynamic threshold based on recent posts (default: about the 75th percentile in the last 24 hours, minimum 15 upvotes).
- You need at least 5 posts that meet the current threshold.
- NSFW, hidden, or unapproved posts do not count.
- Thresholds are configurable and can change as activity shifts.

## Badges
- Badges are curated and granted by admins.
- A badge can have a custom name, description, and optional reason.
- Badges appear on your profile and in the badge viewer.
- Badges can be revoked at any time by admins.
