# Feed, filters, and discovery

## How the feed is built
The feed mixes projects and questions. By default it is ordered by recency. Hidden or unapproved content and content from banned users do not appear in the public feed.

## Filters
- All: newest content first.
- Best: sorted by upvote score, then recency.
- Fresh: posts from the last 180 minutes.
- Reading: posts with 8+ minutes of read time.

## Top projects
The sidebar shows the top 4 projects by upvotes. The list refreshes periodically.

## Tags
Popular tags are collected from recent posts. Clicking a tag filters the feed to that topic.

## Search
Global search indexes recent posts, questions, tags, and user profiles (up to about 200 items per type). Use the search button in the top bar and type keywords or a #tag.

## Showcase
Showcase is a curated set of project collections available at /showcase.
