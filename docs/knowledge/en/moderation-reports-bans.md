# Moderation, reports, bans, and rules

## Service rules
All members must follow the Terms of Service and Community Guidelines. These rules cover respectful behavior, allowed content, and safety expectations.

## Reports
You can report posts, questions, comments, reviews, or profiles. Reasons include spam, abuse, offtopic, and other. You cannot report your own content. Reports are weighted by role and account trust, and enough report weight can auto-hide content from the public feed.

## Automated and manual moderation
Text moderation checks for low-quality or spammy content. Flagged content may be hidden and placed in a pending review state. Media uploads can also be flagged. Moderators can hide, restore, or review content manually.

## Bans
Admins can ban or unban accounts. Banned users cannot publish or interact, and their content is removed from public feeds while the ban is active.
