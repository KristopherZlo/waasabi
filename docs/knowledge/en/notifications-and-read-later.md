# Notifications and read later

## Notifications
Notifications appear in the top bar and on /notifications. They are split into New and Read tabs. Use "Mark as read" to move items to Read. Typical notifications include comments, reviews, follows, support replies, and badge awards.

## Read later
Use the bookmark/save action on a post or question to add it to Read Later. Saved items are stored on your account and appear on /read-later. Toggle save again to remove an item.
