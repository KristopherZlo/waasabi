# Comments, reviews, and upvotes

## Comments
Comments live under posts and questions. You can reply to a comment to create threads. Comments support an optional section label and are moderated for quality and safety.

## Reviews
Reviews are structured feedback on projects and contain three blocks: Improve, Why, and How. Only Makers can submit reviews. Reviews appear on project pages and can be moderated or hidden.

## Upvotes
Upvotes are a one-click way to support posts and questions. Each user can toggle one upvote per post. Upvotes affect ranking (Best feed, Top projects) and Maker promotion.
