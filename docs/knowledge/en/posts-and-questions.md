# Posts and questions

## What is a post?
Posts are project updates or long-form writeups. A post includes a title, subtitle, status (in progress, done, or paused), tags, and a formatted body. Posts appear on the main feed and on their own project page.

## What is a question?
Questions are short Q&A threads. They show in the feed and in the Questions tab, and other users can reply in the thread.

## Writing posts and questions
- Go to /publish and choose Post or Question.
- Posts require a title, subtitle, and body. Questions require a title and a question body.
- Add up to 5 tags (comma-separated).
- Add coauthors by @slug when needed.
- Use the editor to add headings, lists, tables, code, quotes, links, and images.
- Read time is calculated from word count (about 200 words per minute).
- Content is moderated. Low-quality text or risky media may be hidden and queued for review.
