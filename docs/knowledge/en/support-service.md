# Support service

## The support portal
Visit /support to access the knowledge base and your support tickets. The search box helps you find articles fast.

## Ticket types
Tickets are categorized as Question, Bug report, or Complaint/abuse. Pick the type that best matches your request.

## Opening a ticket
Opening a ticket requires a verified account and a minimum account age. Banned users cannot open tickets. Provide a clear subject and detailed description, including links or screenshots when helpful.

## Ticket flow
Tickets move through Open, Waiting for your reply, and Closed statuses. Support staff reply in the ticket thread, and you can follow up in the same conversation. Support replies also trigger notifications.
